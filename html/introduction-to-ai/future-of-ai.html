Future of AI - What to expect?

An exponential growth

AI is a technology that in only 4 years has evolved far past anything we could have ever imagined. Not only is it viable in almost any work environment, its versatility allows it to boost its own growth.

by Alessandro Rohrwasser

A self fueling growth

AI was absolutely terrible in its early stages. Hallucinations and unreliable image generation, which is why it went viral the first years more as a meme machine than anything else. Yet, with only 4 years of time, it became almost indistinguishable from real art or photos.

AI's exponential growth

Today's AI proves to be an actual powerhouse in programming and coding. Because of its speed and power, it is getting applied more and more in digital and informatics work fields.

Even though it is still not perfect, its productivity far outmatches any human's. It's not uncommon to see programmers being pushed to the sidelines, only tasked to making sure AI's output is functional. But this forceful implementation of this technology proves to be extremely effective, as more and more code is written quickly by AI, and less and less time is spent correcting its decreasing mistakes. The more it improves, the more it is able to improve itself: a feedback loop.

How fast did it actually get?

There are many factors that have to come into consideration. If we only considered AI's programming speed, we could easily deduce that it reaches past 5 times a human's. But that's far from being the only factor.

AI is well known for hallucinating and making mistakes, so there is a whole lot of incorrect program that has to be reviewed and fixed by humans. That alone already takes a lot of time, dropping the actual improvement in speed to a mere 15% at best.

Depending on how it is applied, it can result in different changes. Some devs absolutely cannot work with it and it could even result in a worse timeframe, while some could only use it in some behind-the-scenes or conceptualization and prototyping stages, in which it could result rather viable.

The milestones

AI will become more powerful and more influential with time with a greater and greater growth. There are, though, some specific goals which many AI companies are currently trying to reach.

Fully autonomous AI (AGI), an AI so smart that it matches and bests the human mind across nearly every field and becomes self aware, becoming able to improve itself and act autonomously. Of course, this comes with its own risks, especially with the position of great power we are giving it, such as military drones.

Complete implementation of AI in various fields, including, quite controversially, AI. Take for example Disney, which has been pushing AI into movie making for a couple of years now.

Technological breakthroughs, especially in medicine, are to be expected within the next few years, hopefully finding new ways to reach unknown treatments.
