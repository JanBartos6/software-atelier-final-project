Artificial Intelligence in Our Everyday Lives

Exploring how AI interacts with our world, our language, and ourselves.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an increasingly important part of our everyday lives, transforming how we communicate, work, and interact with our surroundings.

by AI in Everyday Life

How Does AI Interact With Our Lives?

To better understand its applications, we divided AI technologies into four main categories, based on what they interact with:

  • Interaction with the World: Through computer vision, AI can interpret visual information, recognize objects, and understand its surroundings.
  • Interaction with Human Voice and Language: Language technologies allow AI to understand, process, and generate human language, both written and spoken.
  • Interaction with the Human Psyche: By analyzing our preferences, interests, and behaviors, recommendation systems can predict what we might enjoy and provide personalized suggestions.

Combining AI Technologies: Robotics

Although these technologies can be used independently, their potential becomes even greater when they are combined.

This brings us to robotics, where different AI capabilities work together to create systems that can perceive, understand, and act in the physical world.

In the following sections, we will explore how AI is integrated into robots, from those operating in controlled environments, such as homes and warehouses, to autonomous systems navigating unpredictable real-world situations.

Learning Before Acting: World Building

Finally, we will discover how world building and AI-powered simulations allow robots to practice, learn, and be tested in virtual environments before interacting with the real world.
