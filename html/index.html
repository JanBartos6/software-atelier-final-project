Do we really know what a robot is?

Giacomo Demartini

When asked to imagine any kind of robot, people often picture a humanoid from a science-fiction movie, with a metallic voice and clumsy movements.

Programmed robots vs. intelligent robots

Giacomo Demartini

Looking at a competition robot, you might think it’s a machine full of intelligence. In reality, much of its behavior is still programmed by hand. Even an action that seems simple, like driving perfectly straight for one meter, requires sensors, continuous corrections and hours of testing.
